## Notes

`lasR-experimental-dense` is the fastest writer in this run at 45.08 s median warm time, narrowly ahead of `lasR-experimental` at 47.04 s, while both experimental variants use about 580 MB peak RSS. The dense preset also produces a smaller file than the normal experimental preset in this run (261.6 MB vs. 268.1 MB), though `lasR-default` remains the smallest overall at 234.8 MB. For full reads, `lasR-default` still transfers the fewest bytes and is fastest, while `lasR-experimental-dense` improves over normal experimental on full-read bytes and request count. For preview and spatial reads, normal `lasR-experimental` remains the strongest result overall: it is cheapest for R2-d0, R2-d1, R2-d2, and R3 by transferred bytes and wall time, while dense generally lands between normal experimental and the other writers.
